\## GeoDev Lab 

The Question 

Which Wards in Ikorodu LGA are more than 5km from a health facility?



\## Why it matters

It matters because it helps to know the area in Ikorodu LGA that and do not have access to a health facility within 5km. It can also be a source of advice to the development institutions or body that is concerned.



\## Data

• Grid3 health facilities, 

• Grid3 ward boundaries, 

• OSM roads 



\## The data I need

Health Facilities – GRID3 - https://data.grid3.org/search?categories=health%20facilities

Ward Boundaries – GRID3 - https://data.grid3.org/datasets/GRID3::grid3-nga-operational-wards-v1-0/about

OSM Roads – OSM via Quick OSM Extracted for my LGA
